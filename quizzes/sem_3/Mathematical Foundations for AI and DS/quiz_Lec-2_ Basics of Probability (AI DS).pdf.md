**Answer:** A certain event is one that is inevitable and will always occur, while an impossible event is one that will never occur.

### Q3. MCQ [Easy]
What is the classical definition of probability?

(A) The number of favorable outcomes divided by the total number of outcomes
(B) The number of unfavorable outcomes divided by the total number of outcomes
(C) The probability of an event is a real number satisfying certain axioms
(D) The probability of an event is a random number between 0 and 1

**Answer:** (A) The number of favorable outcomes divided by the total number of outcomes
**Explanation:** According to the provided content, the classical definition of probability is given by the formula: P(A) = n(A) / n(S), where n(A) is the number of favorable outcomes and n(S) is the total number of outcomes.

### Q4. Short Answer [Easy]
What is the addition law of probability, and how is it used to calculate the probability of the union of two events?

**Answer:** The addition law of probability states that P(A ∪ B) = P(A) + P(B) - P(A ∩ B), which is used to calculate the probability of the union of two events by adding the probabilities of the individual events and subtracting the probability of their intersection.
**Explanation:** As stated in the content, the addition law of probability is given by the formula: P(A ∪ B) = P(A) + P(B) - P(A ∩ B), which allows us to calculate the probability of the union of two events.
