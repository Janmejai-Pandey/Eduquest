**Answer:** A discrete sample space is a finite or countably infinite set, while a continuous sample space is a continuum, meaning it is uncountably infinite.
**Explanation:** The provided content states that a sample space is called discrete if it is a finite or a countably infinite set, and continuous or a continuum otherwise.
